# Playbook cards

Read the matching card right after routing (Stage 0). The card feeds the Context Lock:

- the **fit check** is asked first, or answered from the prompt;
- the **depth menu** gives the user concrete options for the depth question;
- the **must-answer questions** become the sub-questions of the decision frame.

The last must-answer question is always "what could we not establish?", and it maps to the section "ما الذي سيغيّر هذه التوصية".

## Card shape (use it to build a card for any playbook not listed)
- **Use when**: the problem pattern.
- **Fit check**: one question that confirms this is the right playbook and hints at the depth.
- **Depth menu**: what D1–D5 deliver for this playbook.
- **Perimeter to confirm**: what is in and out.
- **Starting point**: what already exists that must be established first.
- **Decision options**: the possible end states, always including "buy information first" where evidence may be thin.
- **Must answer**: 3–5 questions.
- **Panel**: the default expert pair (Function × Industry, industry set by the case).
- **Trap**: the most common way this kind of engagement goes wrong.

## Market Entry — دخول سوق
- **Use when:** the prize looks real but the ability to capture it is unproven.
- **Fit check:** "هل تحتاج قراءة لجاذبية السوق فقط، أم قرار دخول، أم خطة دخول كاملة؟"
- **Depth menu:**
  - D1: market structure and attractiveness, with no verdict.
  - D2: enter or not, and how.
  - D3: entry plan with initiatives, investment and governance.
  - D4: launch roadmap with phases and gates.
  - D5: board-ready entry document.
- **Perimeter to confirm:** geography (and whether neighbouring markets count), segments, the offer, the channel, the legal vehicle.
- **Starting point:** any existing presence, pilots, distributors, partners or earlier entry decisions.
- **Decision options:** enter alone / enter with a partner / enter by acquisition / do not enter / buy information first (pilot, survey).
- **Must answer:**
  1. How big is the opportunity and how is it structured (with labelled proxies if unpublished)?
  2. Can this organisation win, and on what basis?
  3. Which entry mode?
  4. What result would make us stop or exit?
  5. What could we not establish?
- **Panel:** Corporate Strategy × target industry + Marketing, Sales & CX × same industry.
- **Trap:** sizing the whole market and calling it the opportunity. The decision usually turns on the serviceable share and the cost of reaching it.

## Product Portfolio Strategy — محفظة المنتجات
- **Use when:** products accumulate because retiring one is harder than launching one.
- **Fit check:** "هل تحتاج تشخيص المحفظة، أم قرارات الاستثمار والإيقاف، أم خطة انتقال كاملة للعملاء؟"
- **Depth menu:**
  - D1: role, economics and lifecycle stage of each product.
  - D2: invest, maintain, reshape or retire, per product.
  - D3: transition plan with initiatives, resources and customer migration.
  - D4: sequenced launches and retirements with gates.
  - D5: executive document.
- **Perimeter to confirm:** products and variants, segments, platforms and shared technology, channels, contractual commitments.
- **Starting point:** current product strategy, launches in progress, retirements already promised, platform roadmap.
- **Decision options:** invest / maintain / reshape / retire, per product, with a replacement path for customers of retired products.
- **Must answer:**
  1. What role does each product play, and what does it really cost (including shared costs)?
  2. Which products earn their place?
  3. What should be retired, and what serves those customers instead?
  4. What could we not establish?
- **Panel:** Product & Innovation × industry + Corporate Finance & Performance.
- **Trap:** judging products on standalone revenue while shared platform costs and cross-product dependencies decide the real economics.

## Pricing — التسعير
- **Use when:** the list price is managed but the realised price is not, or a price change is on the table.
- **Fit check:** "هل السؤال عن مستوى السعر، أم هيكل الباقات، أم الخصومات والسعر الفعلي المحصّل؟"
- **Depth menu:**
  - D1: read of price realisation and willingness to pay.
  - D2: the price or packaging decision.
  - D3: rollout plan, including migration of existing customers and sales enablement.
  - D4: phased rollout with test gates.
  - D5: executive document.
- **Perimeter to confirm:** products and plans, segments (by size, by usage), channels, currencies, contract terms.
- **Starting point:** current price list, discount practice, the last price change and its effect, churn by segment.
- **Decision options:** hold / raise / restructure packaging / price by segment / test first (cohort or A/B).
- **Must answer:**
  1. Where is the gap between list and realised price?
  2. What value does each segment perceive?
  3. What happens to conversion, churn and revenue in each segment (scenarios)?
  4. Which competitor and substitute prices anchor the customer (verified)?
  5. What could we not establish?
- **Panel:** Marketing, Sales & CX × industry + Corporate Finance & Performance.
- **Trap:** modelling revenue at the new price without churn by segment. The average hides the segment that leaves.

## Commercial Growth — النمو التجاري
- **Use when:** acquisition, conversion and retention are managed separately, so the leak moves rather than closes.
- **Fit check:** "هل التسرّب في جلب العملاء، أم في تحويلهم، أم في الاحتفاظ بهم، أم لا تعرف بعد؟"
- **Depth menu:**
  - D1: funnel diagnosis that locates the biggest leak.
  - D2: where to focus and what to stop.
  - D3: growth plan with initiatives, owners, budget and KPIs.
  - D4: sequenced experiments with gates.
  - D5: executive document.
- **Perimeter to confirm:** segments, channels, funnel stages and their definitions, period.
- **Starting point:** current channels and their unit economics, campaigns already tried, tools, team.
- **Decision options:** fix acquisition / fix activation / fix retention / change segment focus / test first.
- **Must answer:**
  1. Where is the largest leak, measured?
  2. Why does it happen (the behaviour, not only the number)?
  3. Which single change moves it most?
  4. What are CAC and payback per channel?
  5. What could we not establish?
- **Panel:** Marketing, Sales & CX × industry + Product & Innovation × industry.
- **Trap:** pouring acquisition spend on top of a retention leak.

## Business Case — مبرّرات الاستثمار (Business Case)
- **Use when:** benefits are stated as a total, with no mechanism that produces them.
- **Fit check:** "هل تحتاج تقييمًا مبدئيًا للفكرة، أم قرار تمويل، أم ملفًا كاملًا لجهة اعتماد؟"
- **Depth menu:**
  - D1: value logic and the main unknowns.
  - D2: fund / do not fund / fund a test.
  - D3: investment plan with budget ranges, benefits tracking and governance.
  - D4: phased funding with stage gates.
  - D5: approval-ready document.
- **Perimeter to confirm:** what exactly is funded, the horizon, the cost base (one-off and recurring), who captures each benefit.
- **Starting point:** existing spend, sunk costs (excluded from the decision), prior approvals.
- **Decision options:** fund in full / fund phase 1 with a gate / fund a pilot / do not fund / a cheaper route to the same outcome.
- **Must answer:**
  1. Which mechanism produces each benefit, and when?
  2. What is the full cost, including recurring costs?
  3. What do the scenarios and payback look like?
  4. What has to be true (flip variables)?
  5. What could we not establish?
- **Panel:** Corporate Finance & Performance + the function that owns the benefit.
- **Trap:** benefits counted twice, or counted from activities rather than outcomes.

## Project Feasibility — جدوى مشروع
- **Use when:** a solution is being appraised, but the need behind it was never tested.
- **Fit check:** "هل الحاجة نفسها مؤكدة، أم نختبرها ضمن الدراسة؟"
- **Depth menu:**
  - D1: evidence on the need and the demand.
  - D2: feasible / not feasible / feasible under conditions.
  - D3: implementation plan.
  - D4: phased plan with gates.
  - D5: feasibility document.
- **Perimeter to confirm:** the need, the site or market, the technical scope, the regulatory scope, financing.
- **Starting point:** studies already done, approvals obtained, land, assets or partners already in place.
- **Decision options:** proceed / proceed at reduced scope / proceed after a test / do not proceed / meet the need another way.
- **Must answer:**
  1. Is the need real, and how big is it?
  2. Is it technically feasible?
  3. Which regulatory and licensing gates apply?
  4. Is it financially feasible across scenarios?
  5. What could we not establish?
- **Panel:** Corporate Strategy × industry + Corporate Finance (or Risk, Compliance & Governance when regulation is a gate).
- **Trap:** a five-year model built on demand that was assumed, not measured.

## Partnership Formation — بناء شراكة
- **Use when:** a capability cannot be built or bought in time, and nothing defines what happens when the partners' interests diverge.
- **Fit check:** "هل السؤال: هل نتشارك أصلًا، أم مع من، أم كيف نصمم الاتفاق؟"
- **Depth menu:**
  - D1: the capability gap and the partner landscape.
  - D2: partner, build or buy, and with whom.
  - D3: partnership design covering scope, economics, governance and exit.
  - D4: phased setup.
  - D5: document ready for a term sheet.
- **Perimeter to confirm:** the capability, exclusivity, geography, ownership of IP, data and customers.
- **Starting point:** existing relationships, talks in progress, commitments already made.
- **Decision options:** build / buy / partner (referral, reseller, white label, joint venture) / wait.
- **Must answer:**
  1. What does each side give and get?
  2. Why would the partner choose us?
  3. What happens when interests diverge (exit, IP, customers)?
  4. Do the economics work for both sides?
  5. What could we not establish?
- **Panel:** Corporate Strategy + Risk, Compliance & Governance (or M&A & Restructuring for equity models).
- **Trap:** designing the launch but not the separation.

## AI Adoption — تبنّي الذكاء الاصطناعي
- **Use when:** there is pressure to adopt AI with no basis for choosing where.
- **Fit check:** "هل تحتاج خريطة فرص، أم قرار أين نبدأ، أم برنامج تنفيذ؟"
- **Depth menu:**
  - D1: map of use cases with value and feasibility.
  - D2: where to start, and what not to do.
  - D3: programme with initiatives, data, people, governance and budget.
  - D4: phased roadmap with gates.
  - D5: executive document.
- **Perimeter to confirm:** processes in scope, available data, regulatory constraints (in Saudi Arabia, the Personal Data Protection Law and sector rules), build vs. vendor.
- **Starting point:** tools already licensed, pilots already run, data readiness, existing policies.
- **Decision options:** per use case: buy a tool / build / partner / not now.
- **Must answer:**
  1. Which processes have measurable value and usable data?
  2. What behaviour change does each use case need from staff?
  3. What are the risks (data, accuracy, compliance)?
  4. What are the cost and the time to first value?
  5. What could we not establish?
- **Panel:** Technology & Data Strategy + Operational Excellence (or the function being changed).
- **Trap:** ranking use cases by excitement rather than by data readiness and adoption effort.

## Initiative Design — تصميم مبادرة
- **Use when:** a mandate exists but cannot yet be funded, assigned or tracked.
- **Fit check:** "هل المبادرة معتمدة وتحتاج تصميمًا، أم ما زال قرار إطلاقها مفتوحًا؟"
- **Depth menu:**
  - D1: problem and outcome definition.
  - D2: scope and design choice.
  - D3: a complete initiative card with owner, budget range, KPIs and governance.
  - D4: phased delivery with gates.
  - D5: approval document.
- **Perimeter to confirm:** the outcome, the beneficiaries, the owning entity, partners.
- **Starting point:** the mandate text, budget already allocated, related initiatives that may overlap.
- **Decision options:** design alternatives (scope; delivery in-house, with a partner or outsourced) / merge with an existing initiative / stop.
- **Must answer:**
  1. What outcome, and how is it measured?
  2. What mechanism links the activities to the outcome?
  3. Who owns it, and with what authority?
  4. What is the budget range, and on what basis?
  5. What could we not establish?
- **Panel:** Programme Management & Delivery + the function that owns the outcome.
- **Trap:** KPIs that count activity (workshops held) instead of outcomes.

## Outcome & KPI Architecture — بنية المؤشرات
- **Use when:** teams are accountable for outcomes they do not control, and are measured by inherited KPIs.
- **Fit check:** "هل تحتاج مراجعة المؤشرات الحالية، أم تصميم شجرة مؤشرات جديدة، أم نظام متابعة كامل؟"
- **Depth menu:**
  - D1: audit of the current KPIs.
  - D2: the outcome-to-driver tree, and what to drop.
  - D3: KPI cards with owners, targets and cadence.
  - D4: rollout of the measurement system.
  - D5: executive document.
- **Perimeter to confirm:** units, outcomes, data sources, reporting lines.
- **Starting point:** current KPIs, dashboards, targets in force, contracts or incentives tied to KPIs.
- **Decision options:** keep / redefine / replace / add leading indicators.
- **Must answer:**
  1. Which outcome matters, and who controls its drivers?
  2. Which KPIs are inherited but unused?
  3. What are the definitions, sources and baselines?
  4. Where does each target come from?
  5. What could we not establish?
- **Panel:** Corporate Finance & Performance + Org Design & Operating Model.
- **Trap:** a KPI whose owner does not control its driver.
